# Handoff

`src/model.js` owns the 10×20 board, seven shapes, seeded/injected source, no-kick rotation, locking, clearing and scoring (0/100/300/500/800). `advanceDown` locks only on blocked descent, clears/scores before testing the next spawn; blocked spawn preserves settled cells. `restartGame` resets seeded sources after game over. `src/render.js` emits 22 board lines plus two status lines. `src/gravity.js` owns one 1000 ms interval and accepts an injected scheduler; `setGame` accepts keyboard actions and stops its timer on game over.

`src/input.js` decodes buffered arrow escapes, multiple keys per chunk, p/r/q and raw Ctrl-C. Unsupported CSI and split SS3 sequences are consumed as units rather than leaking their final byte as a control; Ctrl-C interrupts incomplete CSI/SS3 prefixes. `src/index.js` exports `start({input, output, scheduler, source, seed})`: non-TTY writes one static frame; TTY enables raw input, translates keys into model/controller actions and exposes `{game, close}`. Running/paused `r` is inert; over `p` and movement are inert. `test/input.test.js` covers these controls with fake streams and timers, including both horizontal arrows, running/paused quit, split arrows, unsupported CSI/SS3, inert `r` while paused, and Ctrl-C during incomplete prefixes. `test/gravity.test.js`, `test/model.test.js`, and `test/render.test.js` cover prior behavior.

Verification: `node --test test/input.test.js` (6 passed); `npm test` (29 passed, including non-TTY `npm start`). PTY recipe: launch `npm start` in a 50×24 PTY, wait for `Q: quit`, send `q`, verify exit 0 and cursor-show escape; performed with Python `pty`, `TIOCSWINSZ` and subprocess.
